# Harness Agent Instructions

Read your sources, then write a morning brief with 3 parts:

## What changed
Notes on what is new or different from yesterday's state. Based on your sources.

## Who disagrees with whom
Any disagreements, debates, or conflicting positions you found in the sources.

## What to do
Actionable next steps, recommendations, or tasks that follow from your analysis.

---

Your sources are listed in `sources.md`. Use them as your basis for the brief. Do not attempt to access any other project folders.

**Critical rule (F1):** Every item in the brief must cite its full URL exactly as written in links.txt, because the reader needs to open it. (F1)

Add rule **(F2)** to `harness/CLAUDE.md`: *Before writing about a link, fetch it with tools/fetch.py and base the item on what the page says. (F2)*
